# Field & Desk — source ownership and public-data policy
Audit date: **9 November 2026**. The public [operations portal](../../site/operations.html) is a source-custody snapshot, not a live private payroll service.

## Ownership
- **Project Resources**: The existing permanent Google Sheet ID remains the human owner of the approved Field/Desk document catalogue, reviewed project document packets and owner-entered source readbacks. The authenticated Sheets publishing run passed on 9 November 2026; this does *not* prove fresh Drive API metadata checks for each linked file.
- **Fishtail manpower payroll**: Its immutable Wage List 11 source and three locked analytic baselines (M01 reconciliation, M02 date-effective wage rules, M03 signature-only labor and Exact Money Ledger) retain their distinct original authority. M03 is a reproducibility baseline, *not* live payroll.
- **Worker Money & Goods**: Preserve established worker workbook IDs, tabs, permissions, version history and archives. Hardened prototype and worker copies coexist. The Fishtail source authority register explicitly says the 21-worker rollout is **NOT_VERIFIED_FINAL**.
- **Wage List 11 cleaned conversions**: Historical derived attendance, work, wage and payment worksheets; none replaces the source original.
- **Tools, materials, equipment, field forms and desk manuals**: Hold at source identification until owner, revision and public eligibility are established.

## Approval before private integrations
Verify separately that the GitHub Actions identity has scoped access to each original Sheet. A successful read from the Project Resources catalogue is *not* authorization to read Fishtail worker records. Check source ID, revision, timestamp, schema, reconciliation and human approval. Store private receipts and private worker-level processing in restricted systems. Publish only expressly approved, anonymous aggregates.

| Consumer | Safe contract | Never own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost Estimation | Validated work-package labour and resource cost projections | Editable payroll or attendance |
| Scheduling & Resource Management | Approved workforce and productivity projections | Private worker master |
| Communication / Maps / Progress | Approved daily project summary | Individual payment ledgers |

Stable technical identities must be maintained privately where source visibility is restricted, independently of human-friendly folder and worksheet titles. Never create a duplicate source to bypass access or change history. Public pages must not expose worker IDs/names, wage rates, advances, attendance rows, personal transactions or private source folder links.

## Refresh and verification
The static public custody data is dated, auditable and displays a **historical snapshot** warning when older than 30 days. The document publisher also fails closed when an admitted owner's Drive readback timestamp is malformed or exceeds 30 days of age. This is a freshness check on *owner-entered evidence*, not proof of a fresh Google Drive file metadata read.

Do not label any private source LIVE until authenticated source-specific reads, privacy-reviewed projections and publication evidence are observed.
